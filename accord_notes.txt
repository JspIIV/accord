Accord rules whether two parties' independently committed terms describe the same agreement, before it is acted on.

Two agents can agree in messages and still mean different things, a meeting of the minds nobody checked for.

propose_terms(title, terms, counterparty) opens an accord with A's statement and names who may answer. counter_terms(accord_id, terms) commits party B's statement; only the named counterparty may call it, so no stranger takes the slot. reconcile(accord_id), open to anybody: a round reads both and returns AGREE / CONFLICT / UNCLEAR, AGREE only when they correspond on every material term.

Each statement is its sender's own bytes, so neither can speak for the other or force a verdict: consensus issues it from the two statements. The contract fetches nothing, and an instruction in a statement is not evidence; proven on a real round both ways, so no injection can manufacture or break agreement. The exact count is kept; the log is a bounded recent-history (last 60).
